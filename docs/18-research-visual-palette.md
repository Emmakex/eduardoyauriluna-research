# Research visual palette

Official visual palette for the Eduardo Research Theme.

## Palette

- Academic Midnight — `#0B1820`
- Deep Petrol — `#123A3A`
- Research Teal — `#087F76`
- Signal Mint — `#61D6C6`
- Warm Ivory — `#F5F5F0`
- Paper White — `#FCFCFA`
- Graphite — `#293638`
- Research Grey — `#8B9897`
- Academic Cobalt — `#4169A8` (limited secondary accent)

## Usage principles

The palette is intended to communicate academic rigor, digital research and technological sophistication without looking like a generic corporate, sustainability or fintech identity.

Approximate distribution:

- 65% Warm Ivory / Paper White
- 22% Academic Midnight / Deep Petrol
- 10% Research Teal
- 3% Signal Mint / Academic Cobalt

## Component mapping

- Global background: Warm Ivory
- Cards and reading surfaces: Paper White
- Primary headings and institutional dark surfaces: Academic Midnight
- Secondary dark surfaces: Deep Petrol
- Body text: Graphite
- Metadata and decorative secondary information: Research Grey, subject to accessible contrast
- Links, eyebrows and interactive accents: Research Teal
- Verified states, research-system nodes and micro-highlights: Signal Mint
- Exceptional secondary visualization accent: Academic Cobalt

## Accessibility rule

Research Grey is not used for small essential text when it would reduce contrast below the Theme accessibility target. In those contexts Graphite is used instead, while Research Grey remains available for non-critical metadata and decorative hierarchy.

## Dark sections

Verified Academic Identity uses Academic Midnight with Deep Petrol layering and Signal Mint verification accents.

The primary collaboration CTA uses a restrained gradient from Research Teal through Deep Petrol to Academic Midnight.

The hero research-system visualization uses Academic Midnight / Deep Petrol with Research Teal and Signal Mint nodes.
