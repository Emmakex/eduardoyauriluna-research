# Project Overview

## Mission

Create and maintain the canonical academic and research identity of **Eduardo Jose Yauri Luna**, with **eduardoyauriluna.com** as the primary public hub.

The public project has one purpose: present Eduardo as a researcher through a credible, coherent and progressively enriched academic profile.

The website must be understood by a visitor as the personal academic website of **Eduardo Jose Yauri Luna**. It must not present itself as a Theme, Manager, platform, product demo, control system or reusable software showcase.

## Public scope

The researcher-facing scope includes:

- Research identity and positioning
- Academic biography and research statement
- Research agenda and research lines
- Academic and research-relevant professional CV
- Publications and working papers when they exist
- Research projects when they exist
- Research software and datasets when they qualify as research outputs
- Academic identifiers and public researcher profiles
- Research notes and methodological writing
- Contact for academic conversations and collaboration
- SEO/GEO and structured data needed to make the research identity discoverable

## Repository transition

This repository currently contains site-specific code together with Research Theme / Research Manager engineering assets created during development.

That mixed state is transitional.

Reusable product capabilities are being extracted progressively into the separate **Site Manager** product. Until that migration is complete, existing product code may remain here when it is still required to operate or migrate eduardoyauriluna.com.

The target end state is that this repository contains only assets, configuration, content and implementation that belong specifically to **Eduardo Jose Yauri Luna as a researcher**. Generic Site Manager product logic must live outside this repository.

The migration must be gradual and must never break the production governance of eduardoyauriluna.com.

## Research positioning

Canonical public research headline:

**Researcher in Artificial Intelligence, Digital Transformation and Autonomous Systems for Business Processes**

Canonical research umbrella:

**Artificial Intelligence, Digital Transformation and Autonomous Systems for Business Processes**

Core research directions:

- Autonomous AI agents for business processes
- Human–AI collaboration and responsible autonomy
- Digital transformation and AI adoption in SMEs
- AI-mediated information discovery and generative search

The current research lines are an agenda under development. Public language must not imply completed studies, formal appointments, publications or results that do not yet exist.

## Website principles

- Researcher first, never product first
- Academic first, not commercial-first
- English primary, Spanish secondary
- Clear distinction between research agenda and completed research results
- Professional experience used selectively as evidence of applied expertise
- No commercial services portfolio on the academic site
- No fabricated credentials, publications, affiliations, grants or identifiers
- No visitor-facing Theme, Manager, evidence-pipeline, verification or control-plane language
- Internal verification remains a system behaviour, not a marketing message
- Empty academic sections should stay hidden rather than explain internal verification state
- Fast, accessible and structurally clear
- ORCID, DOI and scholarly identifier aware
- Schema.org structured data and strong SEO/GEO
- Clear distinction between peer-reviewed publications, preprints, technical reports, software and datasets

## External academic identity sequence

The profile expansion order is:

1. ORCID
2. Google Scholar
3. OpenAlex
4. Other relevant academic profiles and scholarly identifiers

LinkedIn is a supporting source for recovering and cross-checking professional history, roles, dates, skills and other facts needed for the website or CV. It is not the primary academic identity layer of the site.

## Success criteria

The project should progressively establish:

- A coherent public researcher identity for Eduardo Jose Yauri Luna
- Academic website live at eduardoyauriluna.com
- Public ORCID profile
- Research statement
- Academic CV / CVN
- Google Scholar profile when eligible and useful
- OpenAlex identity resolved
- Additional academic profiles only when they add real value
- Research outputs, software and datasets published only when they genuinely exist
- A coherent doctoral research proposal and supervisor outreach package
