## Core research positioning

**Artificial Intelligence, Digital Transformation and Autonomous Systems for Business Processes**

The research agenda focuses on how artificial intelligence can be designed, integrated and evaluated in real organisational environments, with particular attention to autonomous and semi-autonomous agents, business process transformation, human oversight, traceability and measurable operational impact.

The positioning deliberately connects applied software engineering and digital transformation with research questions that can be evaluated empirically.

---

## Research Line 1 — Autonomous AI Agents for Business Processes

### Central question

How can autonomous and semi-autonomous AI agents be designed and evaluated so that they improve business processes while remaining reliable, traceable and governable?

### Topics

- Autonomous and agentic AI systems
- Multi-agent and tool-using systems
- AI-enabled workflow automation
- Human-in-the-loop architectures
- Reliability and failure modes
- Observability and traceability
- Governance and operational controls
- Evaluation frameworks for business agents

### Potential outputs

- Reference architectures
- Evaluation frameworks
- Research software
- Controlled experiments
- Case studies
- Technical reports
- Reproducible benchmarks

## Research Line 2 — AI-Enabled Digital Transformation in SMEs

### Central question

What technical, organisational and human factors determine whether AI-enabled digital transformation produces sustainable improvements in small and medium-sized enterprises?

### Topics

- Adoption of AI in SMEs
- Business process redesign
- Human–AI collaboration
- Organisational readiness
- Automation maturity
- Technology adoption barriers
- Data and integration constraints
- Operational impact measurement

### Potential outputs

- Maturity models
- Comparative case studies
- Surveys and mixed-method studies
- Adoption frameworks
- Implementation patterns

## Research Line 3 — Human–AI Collaboration and Responsible Automation

### Central question

How should responsibilities be divided between humans and AI systems in operational workflows to preserve accountability, quality and effective decision-making?

### Topics

- Human oversight
- Decision delegation
- Escalation models
- Explainability in operational systems
- Trust calibration
- Accountability
- Error recovery
- Responsible automation

## Research Line 4 — Generative Search, Information Visibility and AI-Mediated Discovery

### Central question

How does the transition from traditional search engines to generative and AI-mediated discovery systems change how digital information is selected, represented and surfaced?

### Topics

- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
- Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- AI-mediated information retrieval
- Citation and source selection by generative systems
- Structured data and machine-readable content
- Entity identity and authority signals
- Digital visibility measurement

### Scope rule

This line is academically framed around information retrieval, discoverability and measurement. It must not be presented as a commercial SEO service line.
