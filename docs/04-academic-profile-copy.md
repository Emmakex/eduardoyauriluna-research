## Long bio — English

Eduardo Jose Yauri Luna is a technology practitioner and researcher whose work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, digital transformation and business process innovation. He brings more than a decade of professional experience across digital strategy, web technologies, automation, marketing technology and software-based business systems.

His emerging research agenda focuses on the design, integration and evaluation of autonomous and semi-autonomous AI agents in organisational workflows. Key questions include how these systems can increase operational efficiency while preserving human oversight, accountability, traceability and reliable error handling. He is particularly interested in the adoption of AI by small and medium-sized enterprises, where technological capability, organisational readiness and integration constraints often interact strongly.

A complementary research line examines how generative and AI-mediated search systems are changing digital information discovery, source selection and online visibility. This work connects information retrieval, structured data, entity identity and the evolving relationship between traditional search optimization and generative discovery.

His approach is applied and interdisciplinary, combining software prototyping, design science, experimental evaluation, case studies and reproducible technical outputs. The long-term objective is to contribute practical and academically rigorous frameworks for deploying AI systems in real organisations.

Su enfoque es aplicado e interdisciplinar, combinando prototipado de software, design science, evaluación experimental, estudios de caso y resultados técnicos reproducibles.
